go-kv

Systems Programming

A networked in-memory key-value store I wrote from scratch in Go. It speaks the Redis RESP protocol, so redis-cli works against it out of the box. Write-ahead log with fsync on every write, RWMutex concurrency, dual TTL expiry, no external dependencies. Store-level benchmarks on an Apple M4 Pro come in at 11.8M GET ops/sec, 85 ns/op, 1 alloc/op. Those cover in-memory operations only, with no network in the path.

Go RESP Protocol WAL Concurrency

prediction-market-analytics

Data Engineering / Probability

A multi-venue prediction market analytics engine. It pulls live quotes from Kalshi and Polymarket, normalises both into a shared quote type, and measures where the two venues disagree about the same contract, reporting signed spread and consensus probability over hand-verified pairs. Read-only by design, so no trading, no auth, no database. Around 3,000 lines of Python and 61 tests.

It deliberately won't auto-match contracts across venues by keyword or date. That approach produces confident, silent, wrong signals, so every pair is curated by hand and re-verified against both APIs. When a quote fails on either venue, the pair gets counted and surfaced rather than quietly dropped.
